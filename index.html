ABOUT

Michelle Birkkjær Nielsen

Cross-disciplinary digital designer working where user experience, engineering and business meet. I turn business goals and user needs into digital products that work in practice and create real value. With a background in QA, I build in quality, usability and technical feasibility from the start. I specialise in UX with human-AI interaction, and have a particular interest in sustainability, personalisation and accessibility. I thrive when I can own a solution from idea to finished concept, working closely with my team.
